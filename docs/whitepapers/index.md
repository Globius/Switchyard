# Whitepapers

Longer explanations for people evaluating or operating Switchyard. They describe what is built on `main`; where something
is planned or unproven, they say so.

- [Architecture overview](architecture.md): the control and data planes, the controller's layers, how a change travels.
- [Security model](security-model.md): threats, principles, and how engines, secrets and people are protected.
- [High availability and the data plane](ha-data-plane.md): VRRP and BGP, canary applies, engine replacement, controller HA.
- [Multi-tenancy](multi-tenancy.md): what a tenant owns, isolation layer by layer, roles, what is shared.
